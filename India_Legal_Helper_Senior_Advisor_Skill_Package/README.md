# India Legal Helper — Senior Advisor Skill Package

Modular Markdown skill for generic agent environments. It supports India-focused legal research, business drafting, contract review, due diligence, compliance, and detailed legal risk analysis.

## Contents
`SKILL.md` plus reference files for research protocol, risk analysis, business drafting, contracts, corporate law, employment law, IP/privacy/technology, disputes/due diligence, consumer/property/banking/regulatory matters, compliance checklists, and output templates.

## Installation
Place `india-legal-helper-senior-advisor/` in the skill directory supported by your agent/IDE. For Antigravity project use, a common path is `.agents/skills/india-legal-helper-senior-advisor/`; confirm the installed version's conventions. This package is otherwise generic.

## Example requests
- Review a vendor agreement from the customer's perspective and produce a clause-by-clause risk register.
- Research current Indian law, citing primary authority, effective dates, and relevant judgments.
- Prepare a compliance matrix for a specified entity, activity, and state.
- Draft an NDA with placeholders for missing commercial terms and negotiation notes.
- Review due-diligence documents and list findings, missing evidence, and required counsel review.

No integrations are configured. This is not a licensed advocate, law firm, statutory representative, or substitute for qualified counsel. It prepares drafts and analysis for review; it does not send notices, sign, file, or represent clients. High-risk and time-sensitive matters require qualified legal review.
