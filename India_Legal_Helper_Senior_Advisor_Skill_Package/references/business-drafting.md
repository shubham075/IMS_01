# Business Legal Drafting Standards

Before drafting, identify parties/capacity, objective, jurisdiction, governing law, transaction structure, commercial terms, risk tolerance, negotiation position, existing documents, approvals, and signing process. Ask for critical missing terms or use visible placeholders. Never invent names, registration numbers, amounts, dates, addresses, approvals, or representations.

Draft in clear, precise, consistent language. Make obligations, conditions, timelines, acceptance criteria, payment, taxes, deliverables, and approvals measurable. Consider confidentiality, IP, data protection/security, warranties, indemnities, liability limits, insurance, termination, transition, records, audit rights, subcontracting, assignment, change control, notices, force majeure, dispute resolution, governing law, and execution formalities where relevant.

Do not assume a common clause is enforceable; verify current law and facts. Explain commercial trade-offs and offer fallback language.

Output: purpose/assumptions; draft; key risks; negotiation/fallback points; missing details/placeholders; approvals and lawyer review.
