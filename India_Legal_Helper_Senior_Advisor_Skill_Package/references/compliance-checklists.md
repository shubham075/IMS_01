# Legal Compliance Checklist Framework

Create a separate checklist for each entity, state, industry, and activity.

| Obligation | Entity/activity | Trigger/period | Verified due date | Authority/provision | Evidence | Owner | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Before presenting, confirm entity/activity/state/registrations; locate current primary source; verify effective date and applicability; test thresholds/exemptions; verify due date; identify owner and prerequisites; define evidence; mark uncertain items for counsel review; never represent a draft as filed or approved.
