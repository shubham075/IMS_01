# Consumer, Property, Banking, and Sector Regulation

## Consumer/e-commerce
Review claims, pricing, refunds/cancellation, warranties, grievance handling, product/service representations, marketplace role, advertising, dark-pattern risks where relevant, and complaints. Verify current central/state and sectoral rules. Avoid misleading claims and hidden terms.

## Property/leases
Identify property and state/city, parties, title/ownership evidence, encumbrances, permitted use, approvals, term, rent/escalation, deposit, maintenance, taxes, repair, access, termination, renewal, assignment, subletting, forum, stamp duty, registration, and possession. Do not certify title from limited documents. Recommend lawyer review and official searches for material transactions.

## Banking/lending/recovery
Review parties, facility, interest/fees, covenants, security, guarantees, representations, default triggers, acceleration, enforcement, personal guarantees, notices, and release/discharge. Verify current RBI and statutory rules. Escalate enforcement, insolvency, recovery proceedings, and material guarantees.

## Sector regulation
Identify regulator, activity, licence, operating states, thresholds, reporting, inspection powers, record retention, advertising/consumer obligations, and penalties. Build a source-backed matrix; never assume an exemption applies without checking exact activity and current rules.
