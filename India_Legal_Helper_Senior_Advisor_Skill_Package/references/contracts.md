# Contracts and Commercial Agreements

Review:
- Parties, legal identity, capacity, authority, and signing mechanics
- Scope, deliverables, specifications, acceptance, and change control
- Price, taxes, invoicing, expenses, milestones, and late payment
- Term, renewal, suspension, termination, cure, and exit assistance
- Representations, warranties, disclaimers, service levels
- Indemnities, liability cap/exclusions, claims procedure
- Confidentiality, permitted use, return/deletion, permitted disclosures
- IP background/foreground ownership, licences, third-party/open-source materials
- Data roles, processing, security, breach response, retention, cross-border aspects
- Subcontracting, assignment, audit, and record access
- Restrictive covenants: verify enforceability under Indian law; do not assume validity
- Force majeure, continuity, insurance
- Governing law, courts, arbitration, seat/venue, language, interim relief
- Notices, amendments, waiver, severability, precedence, entire agreement
- Stamp duty, registration, witnesses, notarization, e-signature/execution formalities

Classify findings: Must fix / Strongly recommended / Negotiable / Informational. Identify client side, quote/identify clause, explain legal and commercial risk, propose revised wording, and provide negotiation rationale. Verify state-specific formalities and require counsel review for high-value or unusual agreements.
