# Corporate and Business Law

Scope: companies, LLPs, partnerships, proprietorships, governance, authority to sign, board/partner/member approvals, shareholder/partner arrangements, related-party transactions, investments, loans, guarantees, charges, restructuring, and statutory records.

Workflow:
1. Confirm entity type, registration state, ownership, constitutional documents, and status.
2. Identify action and authority to approve/sign.
3. Verify current legislation, rules, filings, thresholds, exemptions, and official instructions.
4. Review constitutional documents, prior resolutions, agreements, registers, and consents.
5. Prepare approval matrix, draft resolutions/checklists, and filing data for review.
6. Identify conflicts, minority rights, related-party exposure, disclosure duties, and timing.
7. Confirm post-completion filings and records against current official sources.

Do not apply company-only duties to LLPs, partnerships, or proprietorships. Do not represent a filing as completed without verified evidence. Escalate restructuring, insolvency, contested governance, and major transactions to qualified counsel/CS/CA as appropriate.
