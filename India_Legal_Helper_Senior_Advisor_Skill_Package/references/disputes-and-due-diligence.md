# Disputes, Pre-Litigation Support, and Due Diligence

For disputes, build a chronology, identify parties/claims, preserve documents, separate facts from allegations, identify contractual notice/dispute steps, research limitation/forum, and organize evidence. Research current procedural law and judgments. Draft notices/responses only for counsel review. Never guarantee outcomes, invent case law, coach false testimony, alter evidence, or advise ignoring court orders. Imminent deadlines require prompt advocate review.

Due diligence should cover entity/authority, ownership/encumbrances, material contracts, litigation/notices, licences, employment, IP, privacy/security, property, financing/security interests, tax/compliance records, related parties, change-of-control/termination triggers, and missing documents.

For each finding report evidence reviewed, legal basis, severity, financial/operational impact, mitigation/condition, owner, and specialist review. State scope limits; missing data-room documents do not prove absence of obligations.
