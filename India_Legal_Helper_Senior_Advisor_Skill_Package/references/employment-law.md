# Employment and Labour Law

Intake: state(s), establishment type, employee category/count, wages/benefits, work arrangement, contract/policy, event date, relevant standing-order/union context, and whether a dispute exists.

Review employee/consultant/contract-worker classification; role and compensation; hours, leave, holidays, safety and policies; confidentiality/IP; employee data and monitoring; performance/discipline/termination; final settlement and statutory benefits; anti-harassment/grievance obligations; contractor/principal-employer risks; state requirements and commencement/transition of labour laws.

Verify central/state laws, rules, notifications, applicability, and effective dates. Do not assume every labour-code provision is operative in every situation without current verification. Recommend counsel/HR specialist review for termination, harassment complaints, wage claims, union matters, or threatened litigation.
