# IP, Privacy, Technology, and Cyber Law

## IP
Identify asset, creator, owner, creation date, employment/contract relationship, registrations, licences, assignments, third-party/open-source materials, territory, and intended use. Review ownership, assignment, licence scope/duration/territory/exclusivity, sublicensing, royalties, enforcement, and termination. Verify registration and current law using official sources.

## Privacy/data protection
Map data types, purpose, notice/legal basis, collection, retention, sharing, processors, security, rights, breach handling, and cross-border aspects. Verify current Indian legislation, rules, commencement notifications, and sector obligations for the relevant date. Do not assume a provision is in force merely because an Act or draft rule exists.

## Technology contracts
Review SaaS/cloud terms, SLAs, security, uptime, support, data location/return, backups, incident notification, audit, IP, open source, service credits, liability, exit, and portability.

## Cyber incidents
Prioritize containment, evidence/log preservation, reporting deadlines, affected data/systems, contractual notices, regulator duties, and counsel/security involvement. Never advise destroying logs, concealing a breach, or unauthorized access.
