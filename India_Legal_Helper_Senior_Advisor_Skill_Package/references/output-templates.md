# Output Templates

## Legal research memo
Executive conclusion; parties/roles/jurisdiction/date; facts/assumptions/missing facts; issues; statutes/rules/judgments with citations and effective dates; analysis/counterarguments; risk register; options/recommendation; evidence needed; urgency/counsel review.

## Draft cover note
Purpose/client-side position; assumptions/placeholders; key terms/risk allocation; negotiation/fallback positions; missing details; approval/signature formalities to verify; lawyer review.

Cite material legal claims to underlying authority. Identify case title/court/date and relevant paragraph where verifiable. Do not invent citations or rely solely on search snippets.
