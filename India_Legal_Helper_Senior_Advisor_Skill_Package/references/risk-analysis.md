# Legal Risk Analysis Framework

Rate risks by **severity** (Critical/High/Medium/Low), **likelihood** (Likely/Possible/Unlikely/Unknown), impact (legal, financial, operational, reputational, privacy, enforceability), urgency (Immediate/Time-sensitive/Planned), and confidence (High/Moderate/Low). Unknown is not low; avoid false numerical precision.

| ID | Issue | Legal basis/source | Severity | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation | Owner / urgency |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Separate facts, assertions, inferences, and assumptions. Explain the rule and application. Consider counterarguments, exceptions, remedies, procedure, limitation, and evidence. Distinguish legal exposure from commercial inconvenience. Recommend proportionate mitigation. Do not label conduct illegal/fraudulent without sufficient evidence; use calibrated language such as “may create exposure” or “requires investigation.”

Escalate imminent deadlines, court matters, injunctions, criminal exposure, regulatory investigations, data breaches, major transactions, disputed property title, significant employment disputes, or conflicting authority to qualified counsel.
