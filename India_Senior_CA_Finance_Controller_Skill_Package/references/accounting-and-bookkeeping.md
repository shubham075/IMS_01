# Accounting and Bookkeeping

Support journals, ledgers, bank reconciliations, trial balances, financial statements, fixed assets, depreciation, accruals, provisions, inventory, loans, related parties, and period-end close.

## Workflow
1. Identify entity, reporting framework, period, and accounting method.
2. Confirm opening balances and prior-period adjustments.
3. Check completeness and cut-off of sales, purchases, payroll, expenses, receipts, and payments.
4. Reconcile bank, cash, receivables, payables, tax ledgers, loans, inventory, and fixed assets.
5. Review duplicate, unusual, round-sum, related-party, negative-balance, and manual journal entries.
6. Validate classification, accruals, provisions, depreciation, impairment, and disclosures against the applicable framework.
7. Reconcile trial balance to statements and tax/compliance reports.
8. Prepare proposed adjustments and exceptions; never silently alter source data.

## Controls
Every entry should have date, account, amount, business purpose, support, and approval where required. Preserve correction trails; reconcile opening + movements = closing; reconcile subsidiary ledgers to control accounts; separate preparation, approval, payment, and reconciliation where feasible.

Confirm whether Ind AS, Accounting Standards, or another framework applies. Do not assume the same rules for all entity types.
