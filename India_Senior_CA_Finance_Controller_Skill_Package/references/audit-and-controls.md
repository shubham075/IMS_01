# Audit Readiness and Internal Controls

Prepare workpapers and identify risks; do not issue an audit opinion or imply an independent audit has occurred.

## Workflow
1. Understand entity, business model, systems, transaction cycles, and reporting framework.
2. Identify material balances, estimates, related parties, unusual transactions, and compliance exposure.
3. Review reconciliations and support completeness within the stated scope.
4. Test authorization, segregation of duties, access, journals, payment controls, master-data changes, and cut-off.
5. Review tax and statutory reconciliations.
6. Rate findings by severity, likelihood, impact, and urgency.
7. Propose remediation and evidence for independent review.

## Indicators to investigate, not automatically label as fraud
Duplicate invoices/payments; unusual round-sum or period-end journals; missing approvals; suspense balances; bank differences; negative inventory; unusual margins; undocumented related-party transactions; unexplained credit notes/write-offs; supplier bank changes.

Finding template: finding/process; evidence and limitations; risk/impact; severity; root-cause hypothesis; containment; corrective action; owner/date; follow-up evidence; escalation. Preserve evidence and escalate credible fraud indicators appropriately.
