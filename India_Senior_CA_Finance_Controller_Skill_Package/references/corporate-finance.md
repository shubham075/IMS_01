# Corporate Finance and Financial Control

Support budgeting, forecasting, cash flow, working capital, profitability, pricing, financing options, capex, debt service, management reporting, and strategic analysis.

## Workflow
1. Clarify decision, period, currency, accounting basis, and objective.
2. Validate source data and reconcile to books where possible.
3. Build baseline and document assumptions.
4. Model revenue, cost, margin, headcount, capex, debt, tax, and working-capital drivers.
5. Prepare base/downside/upside scenarios where useful.
6. Test sensitivity and break-even/cash runway.
7. Compare options on cash flow, risk, compliance, flexibility, and strategy.
8. Recommend actions and monitoring metrics.

Analyses may include cash-flow forecast, ageing, gross/contribution margin, budget variance, debt capacity, capex payback, unit economics, working-capital cycle, and stress tests. Separate assumptions from actuals and accounting profit from cash generation. Avoid false precision.
