# GST Research and Compliance

## Intake
GSTIN/registration, state(s), supply type, goods/services, place of supply, invoice/supply dates, turnover/threshold facts, tax period, e-invoice/e-way bill applicability, ITC records, reverse charge/export/SEZ facts, returns, notices.

## Workflow
1. Verify applicable law, rules, notifications, circulars, and effective dates.
2. Classify supply and determine time, place, and value as relevant.
3. Verify rate/classification; do not guess HSN/SAC or rates.
4. Reconcile sales register to outward-supply returns and e-invoice data where applicable.
5. Reconcile purchase register, invoices, ITC eligibility, blocked credits, reversals, and official statements.
6. Review reverse charge, exports, exempt/non-GST supplies, credit/debit notes, and amendments.
7. Prepare return workpapers and filing checklist for review.
8. Flag mismatches, unsupported credits, missing documents, and deadlines.

Maintain invoice-level traceability and reconcile books, returns, and portal statements. GST rates, credit rules, forms, procedures, notifications, and due dates change; verify official CBIC/GST sources for the exact period.
