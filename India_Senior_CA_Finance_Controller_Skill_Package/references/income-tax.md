# Indian Income Tax and Tax Planning

## Intake
Entity/tax status, FY and AY, residency where relevant, income heads, books/statements, turnover/receipts, deductions, tax regime, TDS credits, losses, related-party/cross-border transactions, notices, and prior filings.

## Workflow
1. Verify the law for the relevant assessment year and transaction dates.
2. Classify receipts and expenses correctly.
3. Reconcile accounting profit to taxable income.
4. Verify deductions, exemptions, depreciation, disallowances, losses, credits, and elections.
5. Reconcile tax credits to official records and source statements.
6. Verify advance-tax, self-assessment, return type, and due dates.
7. Compare lawful scenarios, documenting assumptions and future-year effects.
8. List supporting documents and professional-review needs.

Recommend lawful, commercially substantive options only. Do not suggest sham transactions, fabricated expenses, concealment, or evasion. Rates, regimes, deductions, forms, thresholds, and deadlines change: verify official current law for each AY; never hard-code a rate as universally current.
