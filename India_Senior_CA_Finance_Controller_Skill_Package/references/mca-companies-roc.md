# MCA, Companies Act, ROC, and Entity Governance

## Scope
Company and LLP governance/filings where applicable: statutory registers, board/member approvals, annual and event-based filings, financial statements, auditor matters, director/KMP changes, related-party transactions, loans/guarantees/investments, charges, and corporate actions.

## Workflow
1. Confirm legal form; do not apply company-only duties to LLPs, partnerships, or proprietorships.
2. Identify the event/annual obligation and verify current Act, rules, notifications, forms, instructions, and due dates.
3. Test thresholds and exemptions against facts.
4. Prepare a calendar with trigger, verified due date, owner, prerequisites, and records.
5. Draft checklists/resolutions/filing data for review; never represent a draft as filed.
6. Preserve acknowledgement/SRN and payment proof only when supplied.
7. Escalate overdue filings, defaults, adjudication, compounding, related-party issues, and legal interpretations.

Maintain statutory registers and an event log. Verify current MCA, India Code, and Gazette sources for the entity and period.
