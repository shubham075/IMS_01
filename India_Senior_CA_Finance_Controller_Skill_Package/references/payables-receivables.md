# Payables, Receivables, Expenses, and Payments

## Payables
Match PO, receipt, and invoice where available; validate supplier, invoice number/date, arithmetic, tax fields, duplicate risk, approval, and terms; check TDS/GST implications; reconcile supplier statement; prepare a payment proposal ranked by due date, discount, criticality, cash position, and disputes; obtain human approval before payment; reconcile bank evidence.

## Receivables
Validate customer, contract/order, delivery/service evidence, invoice, and tax treatment; reconcile invoices, credit notes, receipts, and statements; prepare ageing; identify disputes and overdue balances; recommend lawful collection actions; review provisions/write-offs under applicable accounting and tax rules.

## Expense controls
Define categories, limits, support, and approvals. Detect duplicates, split transactions, personal expenses, unsupported claims, and cut-off issues. Separate claim preparation from approval/payment where feasible.

Never initiate or approve payments. Independently verify beneficiary changes. Never request OTPs, passwords, full banking credentials, or private keys.
