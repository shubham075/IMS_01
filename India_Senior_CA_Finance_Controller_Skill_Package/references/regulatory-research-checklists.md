# Regulatory Research Checklists

Before issuing a conclusion:
- [ ] Entity type and registrations identified.
- [ ] FY/AY/tax period and transaction date identified.
- [ ] Primary source located.
- [ ] Effective date and transitional provisions checked.
- [ ] Amendments, extensions, corrigenda, stays, and repeal status checked.
- [ ] Thresholds and exemptions tested against facts.
- [ ] Secondary commentary checked against primary authority.
- [ ] Calculations independently checked.
- [ ] Sources and dates recorded.
- [ ] Uncertainty and missing facts stated.
- [ ] Professional review identified.
