# Regulatory Research Protocol

For any current-law or compliance question:
1. Search current sources during the task; do not rely only on memory.
2. Identify the relevant entity, period, transaction date, and jurisdiction.
3. Find the primary source: Act, rule, Gazette notification, circular, official portal instruction, or judgment.
4. Confirm effective date, applicability, amendments, corrigenda, extensions, stays, repeal, and transitional rules.
5. Cross-check official guidance and professional interpretation where useful.
6. Record source title, issuing authority, date, URL, provision, and access date where practical.
7. Explain conflicts and unresolved ambiguity; escalate instead of guessing.

## Source priority
1. Legislation, Gazette notifications, binding judgments, official regulator publications.
2. Departmental circulars, instructions, official FAQs, portal manuals and advisories.
3. ICAI and relevant professional-body materials.
4. Reputable CA/legal firms and specialist legal/tax databases.
5. News/commentary as research leads only.

Do not assume a portal FAQ overrides legislation or binding judicial authority. Distinguish announcement, publication, effective, and applicability dates. A proposal or draft is not enacted law.
