# TDS and TCS

## Intake
Payer/payee entity, payment nature/date/amount, residency, relevant PAN status, contract/invoice, thresholds, tax period, prior deductions/deposits/returns, certificates, notices.

## Workflow
1. Determine the legal character of payment and applicable provision.
2. Verify law and thresholds in force on the credit/payment date.
3. Determine trigger, base, rate, timing, and applicable certificates/exemptions.
4. Reconcile ledgers to deductions, deposits, returns, and certificates.
5. Check official portal records and corrections where provided.
6. Verify current deadlines.
7. Quantify short deduction, late deposit, late statement, interest, or fees only when provision and facts support it.
8. Recommend corrections and professional review.

Maintain a payment-type matrix with provision, owner, rate source, threshold, trigger, and evidence. Never use memorized section numbers, rates, thresholds, or due dates without verification.
